How to Play

Master the basics in 30 seconds.

1

Pick Your Difficulty

Choose Easy, Hard, or Super Hard. The only difference is hoop speed — Easy is relaxed, Super Hard is lightning fast.

2

Shoot the Ball

Tap the round shoot button (or press space) to launch a shot. The ball auto-tracks the hoop's current position.

3

Aim for Precision

Want to predict the hoop's future position? Press and drag upward from the shoot button to aim left or right. Release to shoot in that direction.

4

Chain Combos

Hit consecutive shots to build a combo streak. Each combo level adds bonus points — 2-combo +2, 3-combo +3, and so on. Miss and the streak resets.

5

Beat the Clock

You have 30 seconds. Score as much as you can. When time's up, see your stats and check the leaderboard. Then play again.
